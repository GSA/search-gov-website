We understand the needs and challenges that government web teams face. We've designed our service to meet you where you are.

## Highly configurable

No developers required. Use our easy interface to design your search experience, including what content to search, recommended content, and the look and feel of your search results page.

## Built for government

Because we only serve government, we orient ourselves around your needs, whether it is security, compliance, search results coverage, or the unique circumstances of government web publishing.

## Customer service

We are available to help you with your questions. Your success is our mission.

## No paperwork required

Our service is ready to use. It does not require interagency agreements, payments, or other common procedural challenges. 

We look forward to working with you!
